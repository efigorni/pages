## Important source decisions

- All sources were downloaded from the exact official manifest URLs; extraction used the repository extractor, including notes, layout text, SmartArt and hyperlinks. Total source slides: 621. Source URLs are retained per lesson and in both metadata fragments.
- Source stories that would take excessive classroom time are retold in new words, preserving their named characters and causal sequence. Original poems and visual puzzles are linked through `source_slide`; no image-only text is presented as a fabricated transcript.
- Existing source film ideas are taught through the explicit source notes/plot summaries or source activities. External video playback is not required, and no unverified replacement video was introduced. These reductions are recorded per lesson.
- Bead_Azmi is explicitly a compact introduction to all four stations, not a claim to deliver the entire 86-slide multimeeting program. Seven coping routes are presented; pupils expand two. The optional care-circle extension is five minutes.
- Samim_Filter keeps the five source16 intermediate summary statements verbatim in the activity. They are not relabeled as final source messages (D68). Havila closes with an original-source blessing and reflection, not invented source messages. Bead has no final pupil message slide; the video narration in speaker notes is not relabeled as one.
- Consent source19 has teacher scaffold text around the actual pupil messages. Six pupil statements retained verbatim, with typo corrections recorded in YAML; teacher directions are not projected as pupil messages. Negiot source22 question is a heading, and its three actions stay together as a message.
- Corrections are limited to clear typos: Matchilim במצבי→במצבים; Kita השפעה→ההשפעה; consent חיבור→חיבוק and שלא→של לא; Negiot עם→אם, ולאר→ולאחר, שמול→שמולי. All exact from/to records are in source YAML and pass source validation.
- All source-touch/abuse/online-failure activities use private writing or source characters. No forced touching, public disclosure, ranking of friends, or live viewing of pupil accounts. Safeguarding notes retain the repository help-line registry wording and put a trusted adult first.
